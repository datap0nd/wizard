- **Sell-in**: units shipped by the company into the channel (distributors, retailers, operators).
- **Sell-out**: units sold by the channel to end customers. In these reports *sell-through units* means sell-out units.
- **Sell-through rate**: sell-out / (opening channel stock + sell-in) for the period.
- **Channel stock**: units held by the channel at month end. It is a balance: compare month ends, never add months.

Sell-in persistently above sell-out raises channel stock. That can mean channel loading ahead of real demand (a
channel-stuffing risk), a deliberate launch pipeline fill, or seasonal build-up. Distinguishing them needs demand
signals (activations, installed-base growth, Smart Switch inflow, app engagement), stock age and returns. Without
stock age, returns and an owner-approved threshold, describe it as a **screening flag**, not a diagnosis.
