- **Volume share**: brand units / total smartphone units in the market (retail panel estimate), in %.
- **Value share**: brand sales value / total smartphone sales value, in %.
- **Share gain**: difference in percentage points (pp) between two periods, e.g. 32.0% - 31.2% = +0.8 pp. Do not report
  it as a percent change.

Shares cannot be added or averaged across markets without market-size weights. The panel denominator differs from
company sell-out, so a share change and a sell-out change can point in different directions when the whole market
grows or shrinks.
