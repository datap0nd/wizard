Smart Switch transfers count data migrations onto a new Galaxy device, by the brand of the previous phone. Only devices
that opted in to diagnostics are observed, so:

- Counts are **observed switchers**, a lower bound of all switchers. Say "observed".
- Compare markets using coverage (opted-in devices / new activations, report "Smart Switch observation coverage"). A
  market with low coverage can show fewer observed switchers without having fewer real switchers.
- Switching counts are aggregated (market x month x origin brand x target family). Granular device records are out of
  scope unless separately approved.

Observed switching supports "where are we winning switchers" as a description. Whether to invest more needs
incremental economics (margin, incremental outcomes, capacity); without them a recommendation is a decision to
investigate, not a proven return.
