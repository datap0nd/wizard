- **Active devices (installed base)**: Galaxy devices of a model line active at month end. A balance: compare month
  ends; growth = change between month ends.
- **New activations**: devices first activated in the month. Activations well below sell-out can indicate devices sold
  but not put into use (parallel export, stock reported as sold) or reporting lags.
- **30-day active rate**: share of newly activated devices still active 30 days later, in %.
- **Galaxy Store MAU per device**: average monthly active use of the Galaxy Store per new device.

App-usage denominators are activated devices, not units sold. A missing feed is missing data, not zero engagement.
