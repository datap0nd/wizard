**Why a proxy.** True return on marketing investment needs incremental outcomes (what sales would have been without
the spend: a counterfactual), incremental margin and the spend. Wizard's approved sources currently provide spend,
sell-out (sell-through units), market share and observed Smart Switch transfers, but no incrementality or counterfactual.
A result built from them must be called an **investment-efficiency proxy**, never "ROI" or "proven return".

**Working definition (draft).** Spend-normalised sell-through improvement for a market:

    proxy = (sell-out units in the quarter - sell-out units in the previous quarter) / (marketing spend in the quarter, USD millions)

expressed as additional units per USD 1M. Show share gain (percentage points, quarter on quarter) and observed
switching from competitor brands separately rather than folding them into one score, unless the owner signs a weighting.

**Limits to state.** Correlation, not causation; seasonality and launches move sell-out regardless of spend; spend timing
can lag; a market with missing spend cannot be ranked (do not impute it); USD spend uses planning FX rates.
